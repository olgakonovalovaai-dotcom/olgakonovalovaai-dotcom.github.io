Enterprise Recognition Automation

Redesigning a quarterly employee recognition process — from business requirements to automated delivery.

Domain HR · Workforce Operations
Role Business Analyst · Solution Designer
Tech stack Power Automate · Dataverse · Azure Functions · Python · Power BI
Status Production

The challenge

The original request wasn't the real business need.

The initial request was to build an automation that would monitor newly eligible employees every day.

But during stakeholder discussions, I discovered that recognition decisions were actually made quarterly. Daily monitoring would have added complexity without supporting a real business action.

Initial request

Daily monitoring

Continuously identify newly eligible employees.

Delivered solution

Quarterly recognition workflow

Evaluate eligibility and prepare recognition materials when needed.

Instead of automating the original request, I proposed redesigning the process around the quarterly recognition cycle.

The goal became clear: automate eligibility checks, maintain recognition history, and generate presentations and certificates with minimal manual effort.

My contribution

From challenging the brief to delivering the solution.

I led the business analysis and solution design, from initial discovery through testing and release.

Process redesign. Challenged the original requirements and aligned the automation with the actual quarterly workflow.
Business rules. Defined eligibility criteria, exceptions, and recognition logic.
Solution design. Designed data flows, historical tracking in Dataverse, and integrations between Power Automate, Azure Functions, and SharePoint.
Validation & delivery. Coordinated testing, stakeholder demonstrations, documentation, and release communication.

The solution

From eligibility checks to ready-to-use recognition materials.

Power Automate orchestrates the recognition process, applying eligibility rules and maintaining historical records in Dataverse.

Azure Functions and Python generate personalized certificates and PowerPoint presentations using employee information and images. SharePoint stores the outputs, while Power BI supports reporting and stakeholder review.

The result is a repeatable process that replaces manual preparation with structured automation.

A full recognition cycle in approximately 15 minutes.

A representative quarterly run generated recognition materials for approximately 350 employees, including 300+ employee photos.

The solution replaced a process that previously required approximately one business day of manual preparation.

Beyond faster document generation, it introduced structured eligibility checks, historical tracking, consistent outputs, and centralized reporting.

Key design decisions

Quarterly cycle, history, and document generation.

Quarterly instead of daily

I redesigned the workflow around when recognition decisions actually happen, avoiding unnecessary monitoring and notifications.

Structured recognition history

Dataverse maintains historical records to support eligibility checks, reporting, and future recognition cycles.

Automated document generation

Azure Functions handles dynamic presentations and certificates, while Power Automate coordinates the overall process.

Outcome

A production solution aligned with the actual process.

I helped turn a loosely defined automation request into a production solution aligned with the actual business process.

The most important improvement happened before development: identifying what the business really needed instead of simply automating what was initially requested.
