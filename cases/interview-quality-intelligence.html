Interview Quality Intelligence

Using AI to evaluate technical interviews and make hiring quality easier to assess at scale.

Domain Talent Acquisition · Hiring Quality
Role Business Analyst · AI Solution Designer
Tech stack Power Automate · AI Builder · SharePoint · Enterprise APIs
Status Pilot

The challenge

Hiring decisions need evidence, not just conclusions.

Technical interviews follow established evaluation standards, but reviewing whether those standards were applied consistently is difficult and time-consuming.

Interview feedback can be incomplete, skill ratings may lack supporting evidence, and final recommendations aren't always clearly justified.

Manual quality reviews could identify these issues, but couldn't easily scale across large volumes of interviews.

The goal was to turn an existing interview-quality framework into an automated evaluation process that could identify potential issues and explain why they were flagged.

My contribution

Turning evaluation criteria into testable AI logic.

I owned the business analysis and solution design, translating the existing quality framework into functional requirements, evaluation rules, and structured AI outputs.

Evaluation design. Defined the criteria, inputs, and expected outputs for four specialized AI evaluators.
Business rules. Translated the interview-quality taxonomy into testable rules, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
Data mapping. Specified how transcripts, interview feedback, metadata, and skill requirements should be prepared for evaluation.
AI quality control. Defined prompt requirements, evidence validation, and structured output formats.
Testing. Supported manual validation, prompt calibration, and refinement based on pilot results.

The solution

Four AI evaluators. One consistent quality framework.

The solution uses Power Automate and AI Builder to assess completed technical interviews across four dimensions.

01

Final Decision Quality

Is the hiring recommendation supported by interview evidence?

02

Skills Evaluation Quality

Are skill ratings consistent with demonstrated performance?

03

Feedback Quality

Is the interviewer feedback clear, complete, and evidence-based?

04

Interview Conduction Quality

Was the interview conducted according to established standards?

Each evaluator processes relevant interview data and produces structured findings, including severity, rationale, and supporting evidence.

Potential issues are classified by priority, allowing reviewers to focus on findings that require attention.

55 interviews. 4,499 AI-generated findings.

The pilot demonstrated the ability to run a structured AI quality assessment across multiple interviews using consistent evaluation criteria.

Each finding included a rationale and a reference to the supporting interview data, allowing reviewers to investigate potential issues rather than relying on an unexplained AI judgment.

Findings represent AI audit outputs, not confirmed interview defects.

Key design decisions

Focused evaluators, evidence, and a human check.

Specialized evaluators instead of one general prompt

I separated four quality dimensions to make evaluation rules more focused and results easier to interpret.

Evidence-based findings

Each finding was designed to include supporting context from the transcript, feedback, or evaluation data so reviewers could verify the assessment.

Structured outputs

Standardized JSON results made findings easier to store, compare, and analyze.

Human validation

Manual review and prompt calibration were included to help refine evaluation quality rather than treating AI outputs as final judgments.

Outcome

A structured approach to evaluating interview quality.

The pilot established a structured approach to evaluating interview quality using AI.

It provides a foundation for more consistent quality reviews, better visibility into potential hiring risks, and targeted feedback for interviewers.

The project demonstrates how I translate complex business criteria into AI evaluation logic, design evidence-based outputs, and use testing to improve solution quality.
