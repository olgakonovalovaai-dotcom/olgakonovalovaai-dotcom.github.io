Event-Driven Candidate Communication

Automating personalized welcome letters while keeping people in control.

Domain Talent Acquisition · Candidate Experience
Role Business Analyst · Solution Designer
Tech stack Power Automate · Copilot Studio · Enterprise APIs · Event Subscription
Status Production

The challenge

A personal welcome letter shouldn't require repetitive manual work.

When a candidate accepts an offer, People Services Consultants (PSCs) prepare and send a personalized Welcome Letter.

Previously, this meant monitoring offer statuses, gathering candidate information from multiple systems, drafting the letter, and sending it manually.

The goal was to reduce repetitive work and deliver timely communication without removing human oversight.

My contribution

Designing automation around the right event and decision.

I owned the business analysis and solution design, from defining requirements to supporting testing and release.

Event-driven workflow. Defined offer-status triggers and eligibility rules so processing starts only for relevant candidates.
Data integration. Mapped offer, employee, and applicant information required to personalize each letter.
AI integration. Defined Copilot Studio requirements for generating personalized Welcome Letters.
Human approval. Designed approval and rejection paths, ensuring no external communication is sent without PSC authorization.
Validation. Defined acceptance criteria, edge cases, and testing scenarios.

The solution

From offer acceptance to an approved welcome letter.

Instead of checking offer statuses on a schedule, the workflow reacts to relevant status-change events.

Power Automate validates eligibility, retrieves information from enterprise systems, and uses Copilot Studio to generate a personalized Welcome Letter.

The draft is then sent to a PSC for review:

Approved: The letter is automatically emailed to the candidate.

Rejected: An editable draft is returned to the PSC for revision.

No letter is sent externally without explicit human approval.

Key design decisions

The event, the data, and the approval.

Event-driven instead of scheduled

The workflow responds to relevant status changes rather than repeatedly checking for updates, reducing unnecessary processing delays.

Multi-source personalization

Candidate, offer, and employee data are combined to generate context-specific communication.

Human-in-the-loop approval

The PSC makes the final decision, preserving accountability for candidate-facing messages.

Outcome

Released to production, with approval still required.

The solution was released to production, replacing manual letter preparation with automated generation and an approval-based workflow.

PSCs can review a prepared letter instead of drafting one from scratch, while maintaining control over what candidates receive.

The project demonstrates how I design event-driven automation that balances operational efficiency, AI capabilities, and human oversight.
